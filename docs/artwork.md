# Original cover

`app/assets/solar-dawn.jpg` is an original AI-generated cover created on
2026-10-31 with the built-in image generation tool, then encoded as JPEG at
quality 75. It contains no game artwork. The product was subsequently renamed to SECL (SUPREME ETERNITY CRAFT LAUNCHER).
The original generation prompt is preserved below. The earlier planetary cover is not included.

Prompt:

> Create a brand-new original widescreen desktop launcher cover illustration,
> 16:9, high quality matte painted landscape, calm elegant atmosphere. Theme
> SOLAR: a small radiant golden sun just above the horizon in the RIGHT half,
> warm pale peach sunlight spilling through layers of blue-grey clouds and
> distant mountain silhouettes, a tranquil high-altitude sea of clouds,
> sophisticated warm amber versus muted slate blue. No planets, no crescent
> moon, no rings, no Minecraft blocks, no gaming IP, no logos, absolutely no
> text. Left third is visually quiet dark slate blue with soft sky gradient so
> white typography can overlay. Right side carries fine soft lighting and
> depth. Restrained, cinematic, hopeful dawn rather than science fiction space
> art. Asset for Solar Craft Launcher, independent unofficial launcher.
> Clean horizon, no people, no buildings, no UI mockup, no frames.


## README banners

`assets/branding/readme-banner-light.svg` and `readme-banner-dark.svg` are
original vector layouts created for SECL on 2026-10-31. They use the project's
sun symbol, layered hills and outlined lettering. They contain no external
fonts, scripts, linked images or Minecraft artwork. The alternate banner follows
the reader's light/dark color preference on GitHub.
